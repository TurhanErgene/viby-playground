How to play

  1. Hold anywhere to charge. The power bar sweeps up and back — let go when it reads where you want it.
  2. Drag while holding to aim: sideways to turn, up and down for the angle. The dotted arc shows where the stone will land.
  3. Release to throw. The camera falls in behind the stone.
  4. Tap as it touches the water for a Perfect skip. Chain them for a combo.
  5. Drag left and right in flight to steer through the rings.

Flat throws skip furthest. Gold rings are worth 1, blue gems 5 — but gems float high, so you have to give up a little flatness to reach them. Spend the haul on upgrades between throws.
