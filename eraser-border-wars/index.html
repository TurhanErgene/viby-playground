How the schoolyard played it

One eraser, red on one side, blue on the other. No dice, no board — just a map you drew yourselves.

01

Drag your finger across the rival tiles you want, starting on one that touches your border. One tap takes one tile. By eye — nobody measured anything.

02

Your bite stops at 15% of what you already hold. This is the part the loudest kid used to shout about.

03

Flip the eraser. Red + and the land is yours. Blue − and you hand over the same amount from your own side instead.

04

Land on the narrow rim and you draw a power: Double, Shield or Spill. Rare. Worth the shouting.

05

Lose every tile and you're out. Last map-holder standing wins the recess.

New map

Fresh country, fresh borders. Everyone starts with an even share.
