How to play
  • Press a tile and drag to carve out a region — it paints tile by tile under your finger.
  • Every region must hold exactly one number, and exactly that many tiles.
  • A finished region gets a light rim. Too many tiles, or two numbers inside, turns it red.
  • Drag back along your own trail to take a tile back. Draw over a neighbour to steal from it.
  • Fill every tile to win. Voids (✕) can never be filled.
  • Par is one stroke per region — the cleanest possible solve. You can always finish; par just says how well.
  • Shape clues: ▭ the region must be a perfect rectangle, ━ it must be a straight line.
