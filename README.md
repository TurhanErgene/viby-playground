## eraser-border-wars

The schoolyard game: a hand-drawn country split between players, a two-tone
eraser flipped as a coin. Mark off a piece of someone else's land by eye, flip
the eraser — red `+` and you take it, blue `-` and you hand over the same amount
from your own side. Land on the narrow rim and you draw a power.

Single file, no build, touch-first: open `eraser-border-wars/index.html`.
